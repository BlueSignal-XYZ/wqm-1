TDS Single-Point Calibration

1. Rinse the probe, then immerse it fully in a conductivity standard (1413 µS/cm ≈ 707 ppm is the usual one). Both electrodes must be under the surface.

2. Wait 60 seconds for the live reading below to settle.

3. Press Use this value, enter the standard's ppm, and save.
