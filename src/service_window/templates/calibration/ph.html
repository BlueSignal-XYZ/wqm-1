pH Two-Point Calibration

1. Rinse the probe in distilled water, immerse in pH 7.00 buffer, wait 60 seconds for the live reading to settle, and record it below.

2. Rinse again, immerse in pH 4.00 buffer, wait 60 seconds, record it.

3. Save, then verify: back in pH 7.00 the dashboard should read 6.95–7.05.
